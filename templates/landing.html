From page to playback

Three steps. A few minutes.
An audiobook of your own.

STEP 01

Drop in a book

Upload any PDF or EPUB. Lector parses the layout — font sizes, headings, bold and italics — to understand the book's structure, then lists every chapter.

STEP 02

It reads the structure

A linguistic engine scores each sentence — clauses, dialogue, complexity, punctuation — and assigns natural pacing and pauses, sentence by sentence.

STEP 03

Listen as it builds

Chapters appear the moment they're ready — press play on chapter one while the rest still synthesize. Stream it, or download for the road.
